# Order (Yellow)

[All domains and dual-domain cards](/reference/domain-interactions) · [Domain identity basics](/basics/domains)

::: warning Draft — awaiting review
This reference covers English main-deck cards from Origins and Proving Grounds through Radiance. The role index is based on card text and spot checks; conditional effects may be missing or misclassified, and cards can appear under several effects. It includes cards that may be banned. Check the [current Rules Hub](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/rules-hub/) before tournament use.

Order builds a wide board and turns small units into lasting value.

## Cards that introduce Order

Read the explanation beside each card, then compare its printed effect. Domain identities describe common patterns rather than restrictions on what every card can do.

## Find cards by role

**Bounce** returns a card from the board to hand. **Recall** sends a card to base. **Blink** banishes a card and immediately plays it again. **Negative Might** reduces the strength a unit brings to combat. These are search categories, not a substitute for the card’s full instructions.
