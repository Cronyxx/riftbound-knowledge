## Find cards by role

**Bounce** returns a card from the board to hand. **Recall** sends a card to base. **Blink** banishes a card and immediately plays it again. **Negative Might** reduces the strength a unit brings to combat. These are search categories, not a substitute for the card’s full instructions.
