# Domain identities and interaction cards

Use this reference when choosing a second domain or looking for a specific effect for your deck. The [Domains lesson](/basics/domains) explains how a Legend’s domain identity determines which cards you can include.

::: warning Draft — awaiting review
The index covers English main-deck cards from Origins and Proving Grounds through Radiance. Cards may appear under several effects. The index is based on card text and spot checks. Some conditional effects may be missing or misclassified. Check the current ban list for tournament legality.
:::

**Bounce** means returning a card from the board to hand. **Recall** sends a card to base. **Blink** means banishing a card and immediately playing it again. **Negative Might** reduces the strength a unit brings to combat. The index also includes card draw, selection, rune acceleration, and token production because they help fill different roles in a deck.

## Fury (Red)

Fury takes battlefields early and keeps attacking. **Eager Drakehound** enters ready, **Darius, Trifarian** rewards playing a second card by readying himself, and **Right of Conquest** refills your hand when you control battlefields. **Falling Star** splits damage between two hits, while **Lost to the Sands** can hit a unit or kill gear.

## Calm (Green)

Calm takes a battlefield and makes it costly to contest. **Discipline** adds Might and draws a card, **Spirit’s Refuge** protects buffed units with Deflect, and **Charm** moves an enemy unit. **Defy** and **Wind Wall** give holding decks a way to answer opposing spells.

## Mind (Blue)

Mind sees more cards and wins fights through timing. **Clairvoyance** combines Predict and draw, **Progress Day** refills your hand, and **Stupefy** shrinks a unit while drawing a card. **Sprite Call** puts a ready unit on the board, while **Rocket Barrage** can hit a unit in base or kill gear.

## Body (Orange)

Body ramps into larger units and turns Might into interaction. **Catalyst of Aeons** develops runes, **Lee Sin, Centered** rewards a board of buffed units, and **Challenge** lets a strong unit deal its Might in damage to an opponent’s unit. **Stare Down** uses that same Might advantage to send enemies back to base.

## Order (Yellow)

Order builds a wide board and turns small units into lasting value. **Viktor, Leader** replaces dying non-Recruits with Recruit tokens, **Garen, Commander** strengthens a group at his battlefield, and **Remember the Fallen** rebuilds with cheap units and gear. **Cull the Weak** lets you trade a small unit for an opposing sacrifice, while **Vengeance** and **The Ruination** provide direct removal.
