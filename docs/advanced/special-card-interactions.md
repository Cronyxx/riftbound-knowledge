## Gravity Field: who performs the move?

Assume no other effects alter movement or costs, and all spells and abilities are legally played with valid choices.

### Charm

**Situation:** You control Gravity Field at a battlefield. You resolve Charm to move an enemy unit there.

**Question:** Does the enemy unit's controller pay Gravity Field's movement cost?

**Answer:** No. Charm makes you perform the move. Ownership or control of the moving unit does not determine who performs this action. If Gravity Field belonged to your opponent instead, you would face its cost. ([Radiance FAQ][faq], Charm/Gravity Field question; rules cited there: 412.2 and subrules.)

### Angler Wyrm

**Situation:** Your Angler Wyrm is at the battlefield with your Gravity Field. Its activated ability instructs your opponent to move their unit there.

**Answer:** The opponent performs this move, so Gravity Field's cost applies to them. If they decline or cannot pay, their unit does not move. Compare the actor named by the instruction with the Charm case. ([Radiance FAQ][faq], Angler Wyrm/Gravity Field question; rules [204.4](/reference/core-rules#rule-204-4) and [412.2](/reference/core-rules#rule-412-2) as cited by Riot.)

## Runeblade Anvil: when does permission expire?

**Situation:** You kill Runeblade Anvil during the opponent's turn and obtain its permission to play the banished card until your next Ending Phase.

**Answer:** The permission expires when that phase begins. It does not include that Ending Phase. If Anvil dies during your current Ending Phase, the next one is on your next turn. Other play requirements still apply. ([Radiance FAQ][faq], Runeblade Anvil questions.)

Write down whose turn it is, locations, controllers, the action's performer, targets, costs, and the exact permission duration. A change to one of these can change the result.
