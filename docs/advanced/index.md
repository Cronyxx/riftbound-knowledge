## Article format

Start with a precise question and enough game context to reproduce the situation. Explain each decision in order, cite the rules supporting it, and identify relevant card text and errata. Keep unresolved questions explicitly unresolved.

The user selected this mechanics and official-FAQ batch on November 14, 2026. It is an introductory Advanced Rules path, not an exhaustive index of every mechanic or card interaction. Further mechanics and individual card cases will be selected before drafting.
