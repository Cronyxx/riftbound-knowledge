# Start here

Riftbound Notes is organized into four learning sections. Begin with the basics, follow rules questions into advanced topics, explore individual mechanics, then apply that understanding to deck studies.

| Section | Who it is for | Planned coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [01 · Basics](/basics/) | New players and anyone revisiting fundamentals | Goal and game pieces → resources → setup and turns → combat and scoring → a first game → deckbuilding. |
| [02 · Advanced Rules](/advanced/) | Players investigating timing or tricky situations | Chains and resolution → timing and choices → triggers and cleanup → mechanics → combat and scoring edge cases → special interactions. |
| [03 · Mechanics](/mechanics/) | Players looking up an individual mechanic | Procedure → worked examples → edge cases → dated rules changes and card errata. |
| [04 · Meta Deck Studies](/meta/) | Players learning competitive deck plans | Deck goals → dated lists and key cards → opening decisions and play patterns → matchups and practice. |

## Status labels

For deckbuilding, the supporting [domain reference](/reference/domain-interactions) links to separate illustrated pages for Fury, Calm, Mind, Body, Chaos, and Order, with each domain’s cards grouped by role. These references are **Draft — awaiting review**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting approval | Proposed content; drafting has not been authorized yet. |
| Draft — awaiting review | Drafting was approved; the content is awaiting user review. |
| Open question | A recorded situation still needs a sourced answer. |
| Sourced note | A user-approved rules explanation cites an official source, exact rule or section, version, and review date. |
| Reviewed study | A user-approved deck analysis has dated evidence and separately checked rules and legality. |
| Awaiting notes | An older research checklist with no verified explanation yet. |

Community explanations do not become official rulings through these labels. Check document versions and errata before relying on a note; at an organized event, ask the event’s judge about unresolved situations.

Use local search to find published content. Look up exact rule numbers in the [official rulebook reference](/reference/core-rules). For sources and writing guidance, see [Official sources](/sources), [Writing a note](/contributing), and the persistent [site design and content plan](/site-design).

Hover over a numbered rule citation, or focus it with the keyboard, for a short preview. Click or tap it to keep the rule open in a reference pane while reading. A section citation includes its subrules and examples. Close the pane with its Close button or Escape, or follow “Read in full rulebook” to the complete reference. Ctrl/Cmd-click still opens the citation normally in another tab.
