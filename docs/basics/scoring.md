# Scoring and winning

::: tip Sourced note
For a two-player Duel. Rules checked November 9, 2026.
:::

## When to add a point

| What happens | How it scores |
| --- | --- |
| You gain control of a battlefield you haven’t scored this turn. | You **conquer** it and normally gain **1 point**. |
| You still control a battlefield when you score during your Beginning Phase. | You **hold** it and normally gain **1 point**. |

Track a running score for each player. Each player can score each battlefield once per turn. If you hold Battlefield A and later take it back on the same turn, it gives you no second point that turn. You can still score Battlefield B that turn.

## Win by holding a battlefield

Suppose you have **6 points** and your opponent has **5**. You haven’t scored Battlefield A this turn, and no card effects change scoring.

| Moment | What happens at Battlefield A | Your score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your turn | You conquer Battlefield A. | **6 → 7** |
| Opponent’s turn | Your unit stays there and you keep control. | **7** |
| Your next Beginning Phase | You hold Battlefield A. | **7 → 8** |

You reach the Duel’s **Victory Score of 8** while ahead of your opponent, so you win. Conquering for the final point has an extra rule. The [Advanced Rules overview](/advanced/) lists final-point restrictions and exact victory checks under combat and scoring edge cases.
