# A turn, step by step

::: tip Sourced note
For a single-game 1v1 Duel. Rules checked November 9, 2026.
:::

## Start your turn

Follow these four phases in order.

| Phase | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaken** | **Ready** your cards in play by turning them upright. |
| **Beginning** | **Hold** each battlefield you control and score for it. |
| **Channel** | Bring two runes from the top of your Rune Deck into your base. If you went second, channel **three** on your first turn. |
| **Draw** | Draw one card from your Main Deck, including on your first turn. |

## Make plays in your Main Phase

You can play cards you can afford, use abilities, and move units. You choose the order of the plays available to you. When you’re done, tell your opponent you’re ending your turn.

## Finish your turn

In the **Ending Phase**, remove damage from surviving units. Effects that last “this turn” end, and unspent Energy and Power disappear. Your opponent then starts their turn.

## Example of a first turn

You go first and have **Daring Poro** in your opening hand. Your Legend is already upright, and you have no battlefields to hold. Channel two Basic Runes, then draw a card. In your Main Phase, exhaust both runes to pay **2 Energy** and play Daring Poro sideways in your base. If Daring Poro is still in play on your next turn, you turn it upright during Awaken.
