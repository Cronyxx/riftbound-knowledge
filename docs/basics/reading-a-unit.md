## Start with the numbers and labels

| Where to look | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Upper right** | **Printed Might** is the number on the card. Garen has **5**. |
| **Upper left** | The **cost** is what you must pay to play the card. How to pay comes in a later lesson. |
| **Name and type line** | Read the name and subtitle together to identify the card. **Champion Unit** tells you Garen is a unit. |
| **Below the illustration** | The **rules text** tells you what the card does in the game. The italic quote at the bottom is **flavor text**, which adds character. |

## What Might does

**Might** is a unit’s fighting strength. In **combat**, the fight between units at a battlefield, a unit contributes its Might toward the damage its side can deal. A unit can also be killed when damage on it reaches its current Might. Card abilities can change that number during play.

## Read Garen’s ability

Garen gives **other units you control at his location** +1 Might. If another unit you control with **2 Might** is at the same battlefield as Garen, it has **3 Might** while they are there together. The word **other** keeps Garen himself at **5 Might** in this example. **Here** means the location where Garen is.
