# Build your first deck

::: tip Sourced note
For a constructed 1v1 Duel. Rules and format legality checked November 9, 2026.
:::

## Assemble the deck

| Part | What to include |
| --- | --- |
| **Champion Legend** | One Legend. Its domains give your deck its [domain identity](/basics/domains). |
| **Main Deck** | At least **40 cards**, counting your **Chosen Champion**. Choose a Champion Unit whose champion tag matches your Legend’s. A **tag** is a label printed on the card, such as Garen. |
| **Rune Deck** | Exactly **12 runes** in your Legend’s domains. Keep them in a separate pile. |
| **Battlefields** | **Three differently named battlefields** for a constructed Duel. One is randomly selected for play at setup. |

Include up to **three copies of each full card name**, counting the copy you set aside as your Chosen Champion. **Signature** is a label for cards tied to a particular champion. You can include up to **three Signature cards total**, all matching your Legend’s champion tag.

## Try one change to Garen’s starter deck

Keep **Might of Demacia** as your Champion Legend and **Garen, Commander** as your Chosen Champion. The starter has **six Body Runes and six Order Runes**. Try replacing one **Dune Drake**, which costs 5 Energy, with **Cithria of Cloudfield**, which costs 2 Energy.

The swap keeps your Main Deck at 40 cards and gives you one more unit you can afford with two Basic Runes. Count Garen, Commander among those 40 before setting one copy aside for setup.

## Check the format before an event

The Proving Grounds starter supplies **Trifarian War Camp**. For a constructed Duel, add two other eligible battlefields. **Fortified Position** and **Startipped Peak** are examples that pass the domain and legality checks as of the review date. Check your event’s format and Riot’s current legality list before bringing a deck. [Riot’s Rules Hub](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/rules-hub/)
