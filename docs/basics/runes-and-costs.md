# Runes, resources, and costs

::: tip Sourced note
Rules checked November 9, 2026.
:::

**Runes** produce the resources you spend to play cards. To **channel** a rune, bring it from your Rune Deck into your base. Once it’s in play, a Basic Rune has two ways to help you pay.

| Resource | How to get it from a Basic Rune | What it pays for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Energy** | **Exhaust** the rune by turning it from upright to sideways. This gives you 1 Energy. | The number in a card’s cost. Energy from any domain works. |
| **Power** | **Recycle** the rune by putting it on the bottom of your Rune Deck. This gives you 1 Power of that rune’s domain. | A matching domain symbol in a card’s cost. |

An upright card is **ready**. A sideways rune can still be recycled for Power. This lets one ready Basic Rune provide Energy first and then Power. Runes that stay in your base turn upright again at the start of your next turn.

## Pay for Garen, Commander

Look at the upper left of Garen’s card. The **6** is his Energy cost. The **Order symbol beneath it** means he also costs **1 Order Power**.

Suppose you have **six ready Basic Runes** in your base, including an Order Rune, and nothing changes Garen’s cost.

1. Turn all six runes sideways to get **6 Energy**.
2. Put one of those sideways **Order Runes** on the bottom of your Rune Deck to get **1 Order Power**.
3. Spend the Energy and Power to pay for Garen. The other five runes stay sideways in your base.
