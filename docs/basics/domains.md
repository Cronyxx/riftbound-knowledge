# Domains

::: tip Sourced note
Rules checked November 9, 2026.
:::

A **domain** is a group of cards identified by a color and a symbol. On most cards, look for the symbol in the **bottom right**. On a Champion Legend, look in the **top left**.

## Match a card to your Legend

Your Champion Legend’s domains are your deck’s **domain identity**. To include a card with domain symbols, your deck normally needs **every domain shown on that card**. A **colorless** card has no domain, so it can fit any domain identity if it meets the other deckbuilding rules.

Garen’s Champion Legend, **Might of Demacia**, shows **Body** and **Order**. Compare the Body symbol on the Legend with the matching symbol on the **Body Rune**.

## Try a quick deck check

With Might of Demacia, a **Body Rune** or **Order Rune** fits your Rune Deck. A **Mind Rune** does not match either domain. A card showing both Body and Order passes the domain check because Garen supplies both.

For deckbuilding, see [Domain identities and interaction cards](/reference/domain-interactions) for each domain’s playstyle and cards grouped by effect.
