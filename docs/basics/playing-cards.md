# Playing cards and using abilities

::: tip Sourced note
Rules checked November 9, 2026.
:::

## Play a card in your Main Phase

Choose a card from your hand. Make any choices the card requires, such as picking where a unit will go or which unit a spell will affect. Then pay the card’s cost. You can also play your Chosen Champion from the Champion Zone when it’s there and you can pay its cost.

To **resolve** a card means to carry out what happens when it takes effect. Here’s where each example goes when it resolves.

| Card | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Daring Poro** · Unit | Pay **2 Energy**. Place it sideways in your base or at a battlefield you control. |
| **Seal of Unity** · Gear | Pay **1 Order Power**. Place it upright in your base. |
| **Falling Comet** · Spell | Choose a unit at a battlefield and pay **5 Energy**. When the spell resolves in this example, deal **6 damage** to that unit. Then put the spell in its owner’s Trash. |

## Use an ability on a card in play

An **activated ability** is something you choose to use. Look for a colon in the rules text. The part before it tells you what to pay or do. The part after it tells you what the ability does.

For example, once **Seal of Unity** is in your base and upright, you can turn it sideways to add **1 Order Power**. The Seal stays in your base, where you can use it again once it’s upright.
