## Questions to document

- When does movement lead to a showdown or combat?
- Which units participate, and when are those participants determined?
- When can players take actions during the sequence?
- How is damage assigned and applied?
- When does control of a battlefield change?
- How do conquering, holding, and restrictions on scoring apply?
- Which rules govern the final point and winning the game?

## Make examples reproducible

Record each player’s points, relevant units, battlefield, whose turn it is, and any effects already applied. A short numbered sequence should explain both the outcome and why it follows from the cited rules.
