# Mechanics

Choose a mechanic and follow it from an ordinary play through the situations that make it tricky. Each study explains how the mechanic works, applies the rules to concrete cases, and records relevant changes to the rules and to printed cards.

This section is a directory of individual mechanics. For the underlying timing and payment procedures, start with [Chains and resolution](/advanced/chains-and-resolution), [Timing and reaction opportunities](/advanced/timing-and-reactions), and [Costs, targets, and choices](/advanced/costs-targets-and-choices) in Advanced Rules. The [Mechanics and keywords overview](/advanced/mechanics-and-keywords) remains a short introduction.

## What each study contains

1. **How it works:** prerequisites, costs, timing, and the usual procedure.
2. **Worked example:** a reproducible play with its assumptions stated.
3. **Edge cases:** specific questions, outcomes, and links to the exact rules.
4. **Rules changes:** dated updates to the mechanic itself, with publication and effective dates distinguished where Riot provides them.
5. **Card errata:** the changed wording or meaning and its practical consequence. Card-text corrections are identified separately from mechanic-wide changes.
6. **Source record:** document editions, relevant sections, official URLs, and the study’s review date.

Hidden is the first authorized study. Further studies will be added after topic selection. Draft publication does not mark a study as reviewed.

## Checking a ruling

Follow numbered citations into the [rulebook copy](/reference/core-rules). Check the [official sources guide](/sources) for applicable FAQs, patch notes, and errata. Historical wording explains how an interaction changed; the current rules and corrected card text determine how to play it now.
